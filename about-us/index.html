About Us

Dedicated To Quality Service

Your home, business, or pasture is more than just some piece of land. You have a lot of ideas for it–a vision that you’ve developed over the years. Hughes Clearing knows this. That’s why we work with each of our clients to figure out what they want for their land and then provide them with the services they need to achieve that vision. Hughes Clearing is a full-service land clearing, mulching, and excavation company, and we’re here to help you get the landscape you’ve always wanted.

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

Hughes Clearing is dedicated to the kind of old-fashioned customer service you expect from a locally-owned and operated company. We include our customers in every aspect of the project. When we visit your property we meet with you to learn about your vision for the land. As we work, we check in with you to see if you like the direction. We also offer a 100% customer satisfaction guarantee. If you aren’t happy with our work, we’ll keep working until you are.
